Toyota GR86

From Midnight Meets to Mountain Passes

I spent some time shooting a few different GR86s around Tokyo and the surrounding mountains. It's a fun car, and I wanted to get a mix of city shots and some action shots up in the twisties.

Tatsumi PA at Sunset

I chose to shoot this part at Tatsumi PA because there were no other cars and it had minimal distractions. The sunset was super nice that day, and I really wanted to create a clean, cinematic look with the Tokyo skyline in the back.

Touge Runs

Since it's a driver's car, I also went up to the mountain passes to get some rolling shots. Hanging out the window of the chase car is always pretty fun, and it really shows what these cars look like when they're actually being driven.

Shiodome Italia-gai

Later on, we took a couple of them to Shiodome. The whole Italian-style architecture there makes for a cool background. It's just a great spot to park up and get some nice lighting on the paint and the body lines.

Garage Details

We also ended up getting a few shots in an underground garage and out in the rain. Just some simple, moody close-ups to finish the set.
