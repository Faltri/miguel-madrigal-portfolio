Toyota GR86

From Midnight Meets to Mountain Passes

The Toyota GR86 represents the modern continuation of a legendary lineage. To capture its versatile nature, this multi-part shoot took us from the glowing metropolis of Tokyo to the sweeping curves of the Kanto mountain passes.

We started the session under the amber streetlights of Tatsumi PA. The angular, aggressive lines of the GR86 look exceptionally sharp against the industrial concrete and towering skyscrapers of the Tokyo bay area. The goal here was to highlight the car's presence in its natural urban habitat—a true staple of modern Japanese car culture.

But a car like this isn't just meant to be parked. The second half of the shoot transitioned to the touge (mountain passes). Hanging out of a chase car, we captured high-speed rolling shots as a formation of GR86s carved through the mountain curves. The dynamic motion blur and low angles showcase the chassis' low center of gravity and the sheer thrill of driving these machines at the limit.
