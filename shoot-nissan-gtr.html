Nissan R35 GT-R

Late night cruising with the Midnight Godzilla.

真夜中のゴジラとのナイトクルージング。

This blue R35 GTR is just a monster, pure and simple. We wanted to do something a little moody and cyberpunk, so we took it out super late when the streets were empty. The deep metallic blue paint looks completely black until the streetlights hit it just right. We spent the night hunting for cool industrial spots and neon lights across Tokyo. It was freezing out, but the shots we got made it totally worth it.

This text is intentionally kept chill in English.
