Mazda RX-7 FD3S

Vibrant Red Rotary Legend at Daikoku & Tatsumi PA

You can’t talk about Tokyo car culture without mentioning the FD3S RX-7. When this vibrant red rotary beast rolled up to Daikoku PA, I had to grab my camera. The lines on the FD are just timeless. We hung out at Daikoku for a bit and then took it over to Tatsumi to get some different lighting. The car is so loud and aggressive, I tried to capture that same energy in the photos.
