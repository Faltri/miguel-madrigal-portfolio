Toyota GR86 (Rollers)

Aqua-Line High Speed Tunnel Run

Caught some rolling shots of the gunmetal GR86 on the Aqua-Line right after leaving Umihotaru.

This text is intentionally kept chill in English.

There were a bunch of other cool sports cars passing by us, like some GT-Rs. I wasn't sure if they wanted to pass, so I had to make it real quick and grab the photos just in case.

During the shoot, I was riding in my friend's Corvette to take these rollers, and it was a pretty fun shoot.
