Porsche 935 K3

Neon Nights in Tokyo

Getting to shoot a Porsche 935 K3 is always a pretty crazy experience. Seeing this massive race car driving through the tight, busy streets of Shinjuku was just unreal.

I really wanted to capture how wild the car looks against the bright neon lights of Tokyo at night. We parked it under these golden storefront signs, and the street lighting was perfect for showing off its huge rear wing and wide fenders.

All the city lights reflecting off the racing livery looked like something straight out of a movie. It was just an awesome night getting to shoot such a crazy car in the middle of the city.
