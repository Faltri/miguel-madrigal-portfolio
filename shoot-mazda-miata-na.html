Mazda Miata NA (MX-5)

A Simple, Film-Look Shoot

When I planned this shoot for the Mazda Miata NA, I really wanted to keep things simple. The car itself has such a classic, clean design, so I wanted the photos to match that vibe.

Instead of going somewhere busy or full of neon lights, I chose open locations with really minimal backgrounds, like this spot overlooking the water and the Tokyo Gate Bridge. Having fewer distractions around just makes the car look better, and it's much easier on the eyes.

I also got some close-ups of the interior, like the classic Nardi wood steering wheel and the retro gauges. I edited the whole set with a softer, film-like look. It's nothing too crazy, just a subtle grade to give it that nostalgic feel that fits an older car like this perfectly.
