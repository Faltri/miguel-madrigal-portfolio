Mazda Miata NA (MX-5)

A Minimalist Approach to Automotive Film Aesthetics

When planning this shoot for the classic Mazda Miata NA, the vision was clear: create something clean, minimal, and easy on the eyes. The NA generation of the MX-5 is iconic for its simple, pure driving experience and timeless design. I wanted the photography to reflect exactly that.

Rather than choosing a cluttered, neon-heavy Tokyo backdrop, we opted for an open location with a minimal background. This allowed the smooth curves and pop-up headlights of the Miata to stand out without any visual distraction.

I applied a softer color grade to give the images a nostalgic, film-like aesthetic. The subtle grain and muted contrast replicate the organic feel of analog photography, complementing the vintage nature of the car. It's a setup that doesn't scream for attention, but rather invites you to look closer.
