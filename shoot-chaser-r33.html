Nissan R33 GT-R & Toyota Chaser

Sunset Run & Bay Area Meet

Location Tokyo Bay Area
Date Summer 2026
Cars R33 GT-R, Chaser JZX100

Caught up with some friends driving a striking yellow R33 GT-R and an aggressive black JZX100 Chaser. We cruised along the bay and took some high-speed rolling shots before stopping by the water to get some stationary photos as the sun went down. There were a bunch of other cool sports cars passing by us during the evening.
